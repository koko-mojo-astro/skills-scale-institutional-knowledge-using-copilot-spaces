# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This folder is your central starting point for understanding how OctoAcme teams plan, execute, and deliver projects. Whether you are a new team member getting up to speed or a seasoned contributor looking for a quick reference, this README will orient you to our processes and point you to the right documents.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured five-phase project lifecycle: **Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective**. Each phase is gated by clear decision points and deliverables. During **Initiation**, teams validate business need by creating a lightweight one-pager that confirms success metrics, identifies stakeholders, and secures sponsor alignment. Once approved, the **Planning** phase breaks work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates using story points or T-shirt sizing, and a documented Definition of Done. **Execution** emphasizes iterative delivery through small pull requests (≤400 lines), daily standups focused on blockers and dependencies, and a project board tracking work from Backlog through Done. Quality is embedded throughout via unit tests, integration tests, security scanning in CI, and manual QA for feature acceptance. **Release & Deployment** follows a checklist-driven approach with pre-release verification, smoke tests, and a rollback plan, while **Retrospectives** capture learnings and convert them into tracked action items.

OctoAcme clearly defines four core personas: **Project Managers** coordinate schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication; **Product Managers** own vision, prioritization, and success metrics; **Developers** implement features while contributing to design and risk identification; and **QA/Testing teams** validate quality and acceptance criteria. This structure ensures clear ownership and distributed accountability. Communication happens through a regular cadence: daily standups (15 minutes) for team-level triage, weekly syncs between PM and PdM, twice-weekly delivery team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates. Ad-hoc escalation follows three levels—team triage, PM escalation to Product Lead and dependent teams, and sponsor-level escalation for business-impacting issues—ensuring risks surface quickly without creating bottlenecks.

Risk is managed proactively through a Risk Register that captures ID, description, impact, likelihood, owner, and mitigation plans, reviewed weekly during syncs. Dependencies and cross-team integration points are marked on the project board and escalated early. OctoAcme grounds decisions in **data and evidence**: teams track velocity and burndown, monitor success metrics from the project one-pager via dashboards, and measure the impact of retrospective action items. A strong culture of **psychological safety** and **iterative delivery** encourages feedback, learning, and small, testable increments rather than big-bang releases. This combination of structured workflows, clear accountability, regular communication, and continuous measurement enables OctoAcme teams to deliver customer value reliably while remaining responsive to change and maintaining transparency across all stakeholders.
