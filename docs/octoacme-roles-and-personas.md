# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep domain expertise to ensure that requirements, designs, and deliverables accurately reflect real-world business or technical context. They serve as the authoritative voice for their area of specialization throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business rules, workflows, and domain-specific requirements
- Review deliverables and acceptance criteria for domain accuracy
- Inform Product Managers and Developers during discovery and planning
- Validate that implemented solutions meet real-world operational needs
- Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) for domain-specific scenarios

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are grounded in domain reality
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood requirements
- Bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and discovery sessions with Product Managers
- Design reviews and walkthroughs with Developers
- UAT feedback and sign-off with Project Managers

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-centered workflows, wireframes, and interaction designs that ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs. They translate business requirements into concrete design solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize findings into design decisions
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes for features
- Define and maintain design standards and component guidelines
- Collaborate with Product Managers to translate business needs into user experiences
- Partner with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Conduct usability reviews and iterate based on feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction and support errors through thoughtful design
- Ensure consistency across product interfaces

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critiques with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Handoff documentation and annotated specs for Developers
- Usability testing sessions and feedback loops with SMEs and end users

---

## QA Analyst

### Role Summary
QA Analysts define and execute test strategies to validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. They identify defects early and work collaboratively to improve overall product quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans, test cases, and acceptance test scenarios
- Execute manual and automated tests across functional and non-functional requirements
- Report, track, and triage defects with Developers and Project Managers
- Validate that bug fixes and changes do not introduce regressions
- Contribute to CI pipeline quality gates and automation coverage
- Participate in sprint reviews to confirm acceptance criteria are met

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Increase test automation coverage over time
- Ensure every release meets the team's Definition of Done

### Typical Communication
- Daily collaboration with Developers on bug triage and fix verification
- Sprint reviews and sign-off with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Test summary reports and quality metrics for stakeholders

---

## Documentation Lead

### Role Summary
Documentation Leads ensure that processes, features, decisions, and changes are clearly documented for internal knowledge transfer and end-user consumption. They coordinate with all roles to keep documentation accurate, current, and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Author and maintain internal process documentation and runbooks
- Create and update user-facing documentation for features and releases
- Coordinate documentation reviews with SMEs, Developers, and Product Managers
- Establish documentation standards and templates for the team
- Ensure documentation is included in the Definition of Done for each deliverable
- Archive or retire outdated content to maintain quality

### Goals
- Reduce knowledge silos and onboarding friction
- Ensure all released features have accurate supporting documentation
- Maintain a trusted, up-to-date knowledge base for the team and users

### Typical Communication
- Review cycles with Developers and SMEs for technical accuracy
- Release coordination with Project Managers to align documentation with delivery
- Feedback sessions with end users and support teams to improve documentation quality

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure provisioning, and deployment automation to ensure reliable, repeatable, and observable software delivery. They bridge development and operations to streamline the path from code to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage cloud infrastructure, environments, and configuration as code
- Ensure test and staging environments are stable and ready for QA
- Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability tooling
- Define and enforce security and compliance controls in the pipeline
- Lead incident response for deployment failures and infrastructure issues
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows with Project Managers

### Goals
- Reduce deployment lead time and increase deployment frequency
- Minimize mean time to recovery (MTTR) for production incidents
- Ensure environment parity and eliminate "works on my machine" issues

### Typical Communication
- Pipeline status and infrastructure updates with Developers
- Environment readiness and release coordination with QA Analysts and Project Managers
- Incident post-mortems and operational reviews with engineering leadership
